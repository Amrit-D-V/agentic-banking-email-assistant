# HDFC Savings & Current Account Policies

## Minimum Average Balance (MAB) Requirements
- Regular Savings Account in Metro and Urban branches requires an Average Monthly Balance (AMB) of INR 10,000.
- Semi-Urban branches require an AMB of INR 5,000.
- Rural branches require an Average Quarterly Balance (AQB) of INR 2,500.
- Salary Accounts and Basic Savings Bank Deposit Accounts (BSBDA) have zero minimum balance requirements.

## Non-Maintenance Charges
- If AMB falls below required threshold in Metro/Urban branches:
  - Shortfall between 50% to 100%: Fee of INR 300 + applicable GST per month.
  - Shortfall greater than 75%: Fee of INR 450 + applicable GST per month.

## Interest Rates & Daily Limits
- Savings Account interest rate is 3.00% to 3.50% calculated on daily closing balance, credited quarterly.
- Daily ATM cash withdrawal limit is INR 50,000 for Platinum Debit Cards and INR 25,000 for standard Debit Cards.
- Daily point-of-sale (POS) and online purchase limit is INR 3,00,000.
