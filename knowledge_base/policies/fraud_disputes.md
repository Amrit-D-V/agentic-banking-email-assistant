# HDFC Fraud Reporting & Grievance Redressal Policy

## Customer Zero Liability Framework (RBI Mandate)
- If an unauthorized transaction occurs due to contributory fraud or bank negligence, the customer has ZERO liability.
- Third-party breach where neither the bank nor the customer is at fault:
  - If notified within 3 working days: ZERO liability for the customer.
  - If notified between 4 to 7 working days: Customer liability is capped at transaction value or maximum INR 10,000 (whichever is lower).
  - If notified after 7 working days: Bank Board approved policy applies.

## Escalation Matrix & Banking Ombudsman
- Level 1: Branch Manager / 24x7 Customer Care (1800 202 6161). Resolution within 7 working days.
- Level 2: Grievance Redressal Officer (resolution within 10 working days).
- Level 3: Principal Nodal Officer (resolution within 10 working days).
- Level 4: If unaddressed within 30 days, customers have the statutory right to escalate to the RBI Banking Ombudsman under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.
