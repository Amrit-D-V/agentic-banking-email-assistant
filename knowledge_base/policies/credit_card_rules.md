# HDFC Credit Card Policies & Terms

## Cash Advance & Finance Charges
- Revolving credit finance charges are 3.60% per month (43.20% annualized).
- Cash advance transaction fee is 2.5% of the transaction amount or INR 500, whichever is higher.

## Fee Waivers & Annual Membership
- HDFC Millennia Credit Card: First year and renewal fee of INR 1,000 + GST. Waived on annual spends exceeding INR 1,00,000 in the preceding year.
- HDFC Regalia Gold Credit Card: Annual fee of INR 2,500 + GST. Waived on annual spends exceeding INR 4,00,000.

## International Usage & Foreign Currency Markup
- A foreign currency markup of 3.50% is charged on all cross-border and international currency transactions.
- Reward points conversion value: 1 Reward Point = INR 0.25 to INR 0.50 depending on redemption catalogue.
