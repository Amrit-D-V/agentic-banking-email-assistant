# HDFC Account Statement Delivery & Security Policy

## E-Statement Delivery Schedule
- Consolidated monthly account statements are automatically generated on the 1st of every calendar month.
- Statements are dispatched to the customer's registered email address within 24 to 48 hours.

## Password Protection Format
- Bank statements sent via email are password-protected with an 8-character string:
  - First 4 characters: First four letters of the customer's name in UPPERCASE (or lowercase depending on account generation rule).
  - Next 4 characters: Date and Month of Birth in DDMM format.
  - Example: For customer "John Doe" born on 15th August, password is "JOHN1508".
- Duplicate physical paper statements requested at branches incur a charge of INR 100 per statement.
